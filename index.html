iPhone speaker test

  1. Ringer switch ON (not silent), volume all the way up.
  2. Hold the phone at arm's length.
  3. Tap Play on each test, from top to bottom. Listen to the clicks, then pick what you heard.
  4. If Safari asks for the microphone, tap Allow.
  5. At the end, tap Send results.
